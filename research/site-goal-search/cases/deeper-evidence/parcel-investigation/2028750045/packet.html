- pluto — Tax-lot attributes may lag actual conditions. Zoning fields are recorded descriptors, not a legal entitlement analysis. Zero recorded residential units does not mean vacancy or no displacement. Publisher dataset. Terms.

- dob_bis — Legacy job/document rows, not unique projects or verified current use. Historical existing/proposed units are not current lawful dwellings. Exact-BBL queries miss unlinked filings and historical lot changes. Publisher dataset. Terms.

- dob_now — DOB NOW filing rows are not completed projects or a complete pipeline. Exact-BBL lookup only; missing rows do not establish no activity. Publisher dataset. Terms.

- hpd_violations — Recorded housing violation rows, not occupancy, executed evictions, or a displacement measure. Empty results do not prove no housing, no violations, or a safe building. Publisher dataset. Terms.
