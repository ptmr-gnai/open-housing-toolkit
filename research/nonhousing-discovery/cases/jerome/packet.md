## Coverage and limits

PLUTO recorded point lies in closed bbox; not every polygon intersecting it. Unlocated lots cannot be spatially assigned.

Recorded PLUTO zoning fields only; no fresh parcel-polygon/zoning-polygon intersection. Official map, sub-10%-area pieces and applicable text remain review items.

- Recorded nonhousing does not establish vacancy, absence of occupants, site availability or ease of relocation.
- PLUTO district records are not a fresh spatial intersection or authoritative parcel entitlement opinion; small district pieces and intervening map amendments can be missed.
- Special, split, paired, exceptional and missing districts are explicit review paths, not automatic rezoning candidates.
- No housing yield, legal envelope, owner willingness, walking access, assemblage or funded feasibility is inferred.
