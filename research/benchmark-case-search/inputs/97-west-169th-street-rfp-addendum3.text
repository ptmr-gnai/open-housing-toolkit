                                         General Questions

Q1: Is there a particular reason why our real estate attorney must have condominium experience
(see page 19 of the RFP)? We don’t plan on having any condo retail/community facility space. Are
we missing condominium space as a requirement?
A1: The proposed project may include a condominium, but it is not required. The development team’s real
estate attorney should have experience with elements that are in the proposed project.

Q2: The RFP references an E-4 excel sheet for Commercial Management Experience, if applicable.
Where can we find that form?
A2: There is no Form e-4 included with the RFP. Commercial management experience can be listed in
the format you prefer.

Q3: Will any of the questions submitted by applicants and their answers be posted on the HPD
RFP website?
