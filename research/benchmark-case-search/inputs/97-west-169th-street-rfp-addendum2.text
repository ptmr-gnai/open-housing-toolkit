                                             General Questions

Q1: Should RFP respondents assume either 4% bond financing or 9% competitive tax credit
financing? Is there a preference for one over the other?
A1: Respondents may assume either 4% or 9% Low Income Housing Tax Credits (LIHTC), so long as
they are able to show that it is realistic and feasible for the proposed project. If proposing to use 9% tax
credits, submissions must submit historical letters of interest or award letters for similar projects financed
within the past seven years. There is no preference for using either type of tax credit program.

Q2: Can we obtain the contour dwg files or any other digital data available?
A2: The contour map was made using shapefiles of a planimetric basemap layer containing citywide 2-
foot contour line features and is hosted on the NYC Open Data website at this link:
https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Contours/3cdm-p29e. A dwg file is not available of the
contour lines.
