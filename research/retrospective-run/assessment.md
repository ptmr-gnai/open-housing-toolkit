## Identity and condition

HPD describes approximately 15,100 square feet of vacant City-owned former parkland and reports 2017 State authorization for affordable housing (RFP PDF p.8; C01). Vacant is an agency description, not proof of level, clean or construction-ready ground. The snapshot says Block 2519, Lots 27/32 (p.8), but the site-context paragraph says Block 2509, same lots (p.9). Addendum 1 refers to Block 2519 records (p.2). Working identity is 2519 because snapshot and later addendum agree; title, lawful boundaries and statutory conditions require counsel and survey verification (C02–C03).

HPD places a six-story senior building north/west, smaller homes east, and the future park on the remaining northern parkland/Fischer Place (RFP p.9; C04). The respondent’s question about extreme elevation changes and rock outcroppings is a warning to investigate, not an HPD geotechnical finding. HPD cannot provide a survey, gives citywide two-foot contour information, and prohibited survey equipment at its scheduled visit (Addendum 1 p.2; C10–C12). Rock depth/extent, measured elevations and boundaries remain unknown.

## Pathways worth investigating

- **Senior affordable rental housing under SARA, with AIRS review.** HPD identifies R7-1 residential/community-facility permissions and AIRS additional floor-area eligibility (RFP p.10), requires all units affordable for seniors (p.21), and Addendum 1 says assume age 62+ (p.4). A registered architect and zoning counsel should test compliant, accessible layouts and aging in place. This is responsiveness to the brief, not an independently discovered unit yield (C05–C06).
- **Senior supportive variant.** Addendum 1 permits replacing the 30% general homeless set-aside with 30% supportive housing; supportive rental assistance applies only to supportive units, with PBV for others (p.4). Investigate only with credible eligible service funding/provider arrangements. Permission to assume NYC 15/15 funding is not an award. The RFP requires service source/provider identification while omitting a social-services budget from submission; that omission does not establish funded operations (RFP p.28; C07,C09).
- **Community-use variant.** Optional community/commercial space needs a neighborhood rationale, tenancy and management, and rental income or equity covering all associated hard/soft costs (RFP pp.21,23). Compare it with a housing-focused concept until those inputs are evidenced. Condominium is optional (Addendum 3 p.2; C08–C09).
- **Parallel 4% bond and 9% competitive-credit underwriting.** Both are permitted without preference if realistic and feasible; 9% needs historical similar-project letters within seven years (Addendum 2 p.2). SARA assumptions are capped at up to $75,000/unit for 4% and $125,000/unit for 9% (Addendum 1 p.4). Caps do not establish equity proceeds, award, debt capacity or funding sufficiency (C18,C21).

## Distinct feasibility gates

**Legal capacity:** State parkland authorization reported in the RFP is one element, not a title opinion. Reconcile identity, alienation statute/conditions, title and park boundaries. ULURP/disposition, Council/Mayoral and UDAAP approvals remain required; selection only starts negotiations. Remainder interest reverts title at regulatory expiry absent mutually agreed extension (RFP pp.8,18; C03,C13). Clarify the minimum-30-year/regulatory-duration stabilization wording against perpetuity language, without assuming an exit right (pp.23,37,41; C23).

**Physical fit:** Obtain measured survey/access and geotechnical/structural advice, then test applicable AIRS/zoning, accessible entry, egress, neighbors and park interface. Approximate lot size alone establishes neither zoning area nor buildable gross/net area or units (RFP pp.10,24,45; Addendum 1 p.2; C11–C12). As-is conveyance, developer-funded environmental review/mitigation and agency oversight leave contamination and noise/archaeological obligations open; neither clean land nor contamination is established (RFP pp.34,40; C16–C17).

**Financial feasibility:** Obtain full linked term sheets/design guidance, verified rent/allowance figures and comparables, credible hard/soft costs, credit pricing, lender/syndicator indications and service commitments. Addendum 1 specifies November 13, 2014 SARA absent further HPD instructions and prevailing-wage assumptions; PBV underwriting uses 100% of 2019 FMR but the actual dollar inputs are not verified here. Tax benefits require eligibility and are not warranted; nominal land price does not remove taxes or construction costs (RFP pp.21,28–29,34–35; Addendum 1 p.4; C19–C21). No positive capital or operating feasibility conclusion is available.

## Addenda and delivery implications

Amendments govern submissions (RFP p.16). Addendum 1 clarifies age, supportive substitution/rental allocation, SARA caps/sheet and prevailing wage, physical-data limits and site access; removes rail-overbuild applicability, clarifies construction manager as GC, and gives no modular scoring preference (pp.2–4). Addendum 2 allows either credit route with evidence and says no contour DWG exists (p.2). Addendum 3 makes condominium optional, matches counsel experience to project elements and permits flexible commercial-management evidence instead of absent E-4 (p.2; C22). These correct or clarify the original solicitation; they do not certify capacity or funding.

Addendum 1 estimates pre-ULURP/ULURP at roughly 1.5–2 years from selection; the RFP expects construction start within 18–36 months of Negotiation Letter. DPR would review staging only if it ends by spring 2021, with park construction anticipated in 2021 (RFP p.19; Addendum 1 p.3). Approval duration may conflict with that window. Seek explicit DPR acceptance if pursued and maintain alternative logistics (C14–C15).
