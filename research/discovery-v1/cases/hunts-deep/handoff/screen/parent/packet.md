## Scope and limits

PLUTO recorded point lies in closed bbox; not every polygon intersecting it. Unlocated lots cannot be spatially assigned.

Descending incremental zoning-area proxy, BBL tie-break; not community benefit, feasibility or likelihood of approval.

- No future No-Action forecast; recorded FAR is not a legally reconciled envelope.
- No cadastral polygon intersection, parcel assemblage, walking/job access, displacement estimate, market underwriting or approval prediction in v1.
- Zero recorded homes and a COLP NO USE label do not establish vacancy or availability.
- Tax-lot joins may miss historic lot changes; application rows do not establish constructed outcomes.
