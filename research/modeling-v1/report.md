# Rental finance case findings

The new model separates categorized capital uses, source status, annual rental
operations, reserves, loan sizing, debt service, maturity and unallocated capital.
Every monetary assumption has a stated basis, and the packet includes calculation
traces and annual CSV. Proposed sources remain proposed when reused at a new site.

The illustrative 60-home base totals $53,262,500. Modeled primary debt is
$14,054,006.46, leaving $36,208,493.54 after proposed equity. Lower rent and higher
construction costs are retained as separate and combined cases. None of these
inputs is asserted to be an empirical Bronx market estimate.

Flat rent with rising expenses exposes the difference between first-year and
multi-year debt sizing: first-year sizing breaches coverage in years 2–20.
Sizing across the projection reduces debt and increases the capital gap. A
20-year balloon on 30-year amortization produces a $7,589,667.50 maturity
obligation and negative year-20 cash of $7,127,238.92. Scheduled year-end loan
balance becomes zero after modeled payoff, but negative cash means payment is
not funded. A fully paid 15-year loan has no DSCR after payoff; cash deficits
remain a separate test. Combined leverage includes subordinate principal.

Stanyan remains a principal known-case reference rather than a declaration of
best-in-class universal practice. The preserved separate-year controls reproduce
$169,152,004 capital cost, $1,272,978 coverage NOI and $207,183 operating residual.
Calculated effective income is $3,946,019 versus $3,946,018 reported: the $1
discrepancy stays visible. Printed 6.08% interest, 20-year amortization and 1.15
coverage yield $12,793,048.57 supportable debt versus reported $12,793,046;
the $2.57 residual requires the original workbook to resolve. We do not force
agreement or substitute those control terms for actual proposed bank terms.

The 2022 area, 2023 capital and 2025 operating sources are separate dated
snapshots. A control with no imported funding sources does not demonstrate an
actual historical funding gap. The source-based Stanyan/Bronx methodological
comparison is preserved in [the earlier case study](../stanyan-methodology/README.md).
These are known-case reconstructions and sensitivity tests, not blind outcome
backtests. Construction timing, investor returns, tax-credit proceeds, legal
eligibility and site-specific cost validation remain outside this v1.
