# Goal seek housing sites

Use after broad [nonhousing discovery](../discover-nonhousing-sites/SKILL.md).
Read the [execution reference](references/runbook.md) before running the module.

Collect the user's targets and an attributed rental underwriting template. Desired
home counts imply required area under explicit net/gross/zoning conversions. The
search does not require auto use or extra FAR, and it never changes the discovery
universe. Any scenario density cap is the user's search choice, not a legal rule.

All program, land-price and scaling assumptions remain visible. Missing target
evidence stays unknown; point proximity is not walking access. Industrial policy
and Fast Tracker context do not establish entitlement or environmental clearance.
All financing copied to a new site/program is treated as proposed.

Use fresh local outputs and verify. Return matching, failed and unknown counts,
non-dominated scenario alternatives, sensitivity, retained data and unresolved
work. Keep alternatives with different tradeoffs; do not invent a universal
viability/community-benefit score or call a capital gap an available subsidy.
A selected model is not a lawful building program. Deeper handoffs remain
explicit and evidence-only.
