# Licensing and source boundaries

Project code, JSON schemas, and DSL configuration: MIT, see LICENSE.

Original prose documentation and generated prose reports: Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Attribute the Open Housing
Opportunity Playbook contributors. Public source quotations and data are excluded
from this original-documentation license.

Source snapshots remain subject to their publishers' terms. NYC Open Data terms:
https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/#termsofuse . MTA developer resources and
terms: https://www.mta.info/developers . NYC website terms:
https://www.nyc.gov/home/terms-of-use.page . Each source record includes a terms
reference where identified. Discovery catalog metadata is used to locate public
datasets and does not establish underlying parcel facts.

This package preserves limited public research snapshots to permit reproducibility.
It does not claim that an MIT license grants rights to every upstream dataset.
Before redistributing a larger collection or publishing a service, review the
current upstream terms and attribution requirements. No private records, account
credentials, or advisor identities are included.
